Key dates

The milestones that shape what happens to a bill, from the first day of session to the day new laws take effect.

  1. Jan. 13, 2027

    Session convenes

    Lawmakers gather in Richmond, and bills begin moving through committees.

  2. [DATE]

    Crossover

    The last day each chamber can pass its own bills. After this, the House works on Senate bills and the Senate on House bills.

  3. [DATE]

    Sine die

    The scheduled end of the regular session. Bills that haven't passed both chambers by now don't become law this year.

  4. [DATE]

    Reconvened (veto) session

    Lawmakers return to vote on the governor's amendments and vetoes.

  5. July 1, 2027

    New laws take effect

    Most bills signed into law take effect on July 1, unless the bill sets a different date.
