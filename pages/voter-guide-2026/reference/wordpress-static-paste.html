Voting FAQ

Everything you need to cast your ballot in Virginia

Register online at vote.virginia.gov, by mail, or in person at your local registrar’s office. The deadline to register is 15 days before the election — October 19 for the November 3 general election.

Yes. Virginia offers no-excuse absentee voting. You can request an absentee ballot online, by mail, or in person at your registrar’s office. Early in-person voting begins 45 days before the election.

Virginia requires a photo ID to vote in person. Acceptable forms include a Virginia driver’s license, U.S. passport, military ID, or a free Virginia voter photo ID card from your registrar.

If you haven’t yet deposited your ballot or fed it into the scanner, ask a poll worker for a replacement. Spoiled ballots are noted in the poll book. For mailed ballots, contact your registrar.

Enter your address at vote.virginia.gov/VoterInformation to find your assigned polling place, view your sample ballot, and check your registration status.

Rights restoration in Virginia is now automatic upon completion of your sentence, including any period of probation or parole, under a 2021 executive order made permanent by the General Assembly.

Yes. Polls are open from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Election Day. If you are in line by 7 p.m., you are entitled to vote regardless of how long it takes.

Still have questions? Virginia’s Department of Elections has resources available in multiple languages.
